# MONEY: Who pays, and how much, for a student-annoyance AI service (South Korea, 2024–2026)

Scope note: Korea first. Global data appears only as labeled benchmarks. Research date: 2026-10-03. Where a source's date was unclear, it is flagged. Number formatting: 억 = 100M KRW, 만 = 10K KRW.

---

## Q1. How much do Korean university students spend, and will they pay for AI?

### Takeaway
The average student spends about 68만원 a month. Only about 2.9% of that (roughly 2만원) goes to online content, apps and software, but that share has doubled since 2021. Paid AI use among people in their 20s is real: 30% of 20s AI users pay, and 61% of heavy-AI students pay. The working ceiling for any single new subscription is around 8,000원 a month, because free big-tech student offers now crowd the market.

### Cited Findings
- **Monthly spending (Nov 2025):** 68.1만원, up 16.8% from 58.3만원 in 2021. That is roughly flat in real terms, since cumulative inflation was 13.7%. — [오픈애즈 / 대학내일20대연구소 "요즘 대학생의 소비지출 리포트"](https://www.openads.co.kr/content/contentDetail?contsId=20343)
  - Breakdown: food 32.0%, transport 15.5%, leisure 9.0%, daily goods 7.5%, clothing/beauty 7.1%.
  - Online content, apps and software subscriptions: **2.9%**, up from 1.2% in 2021.
  - Fieldwork 12–17 Nov 2025, online panel with quota sampling.
- **Older allowance surveys (date unclear, likely pre-2024; flag as dated):**
  - 알바몬 surveys report a monthly allowance of 44.8만원, with food as the top category (63.8%). — [이데일리](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=02033606599696200)
  - Another reports about 51만원 (50만8074원, excluding tuition and rent). — [베리타스알파](http://www.veritas-a.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=501206)
  - Source of the allowance: 47.0% partly parents and partly self, 26.9% entirely self-funded, 26.1% entirely parents.
- **Paid AI among heavy users (Jun 2025):** 61.0% of frequent generative-AI users (대학(원)생 aged 19–29, n=100) pay for AI. 71.0% used AI for assignments, reports or exam prep. The report puts paid AI plans at **about 3만원 a month** and calls AI a fixed cost in student budgets. Caveat: the sample is small and skewed toward heavy users. — [대학내일20대연구소](https://www.20slab.org/Archives/39112)
- **Paid AI by age (한국소비자원 "2025 한국의 소비생활지표"):** n=10,000 adults; 1,101 use generative AI; 24.3% of those pay. People in their 20s have the highest paid rate at **30.1%**. — [서울경제](https://www.sedaily.com/article/20049653)
- **2026 general population:** a May 2026 survey reports that 9 in 10 people have used AI and about half have had a paid AI subscription. This is not student-specific, and only the headline was checked. — [파이낸셜뉴스](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202605270630351247)
- **Subscription count:** people in their 20s use an average of **7.3 subscription services** (Feb 2025). — [이코노미스트](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202502160006)
- **Price sensitivity on OTT (2025 content-use survey, general population):**
  - Average of 2.1 paid OTT services; paying users spend **10,909원 a month** in total.
  - Perceived fair price for one subscription: **7,939원**.
  - The share choosing ad-supported Netflix or TVING plans rose to 34.8%, from 24.6%.
  - — [이데일리](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=02217286642398848). A 2026 follow-up headline says "구독료 1만 원도 아깝다" and reports a shift to account sharing and ad tiers. — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032000181152827)
- **The "AI divide" (May 2026 reporting):** some students spend heavily. Examples: one graduate student pays 159,000원 a month (ChatGPT + Codex) and another pays 250,000원 a month (Claude Max plus another tool). Students say paid and free users differ visibly in efficiency. — [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260520_0003637590)
- **Affordability complaints (Sep 2026):** student-council leaders told the budget minister that "챗GPT 구독료도 벅차다". They said generative-AI costs are a real burden, that school-provided AI has usage limits, and they asked for national-level support. — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026090321433860550)
- **Free big-tech offers to Korean students:**
  - Google AI Pro is free for 1 year; it normally costs about 29,000원 a month (announced 7 Aug 2025).
  - Perplexity Pro is free for up to 2 years; it normally costs about 28,000원 a month.
  - — [파이낸셜뉴스](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202508090900587591); [Google Korea blog](https://blog.google/intl/ko-kr/company-news/technology/google-ai-pro-students-learning/)
  - Google AI Plus (11,000원 a month) is also offered free for 1 year to students who verify a school email. — [한국대학신문](https://news.unn.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=596374)

### Inferences
- About 2만원 a month (2.9% of 68.1만원) is the whole "apps and software" budget of an average student. Any new paid tool competes for a slice of that 2만원, unless the student is one of the roughly 30% who already pay 3만원 or more for general AI.
- Free Google AI Pro, AI Plus and Perplexity Pro mean a student-annoyance service cannot charge for "general AI". It can charge only for a specific outcome: a task completed, time saved, or money recovered.
- The 7,939원 "fair price per subscription" figure and the "1만 원도 아깝다" sentiment put a soft ceiling of about 5,000–8,000원 a month on a single-purpose B2C subscription.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean survey that measures students' stated willingness to pay, in KRW, for a single-purpose AI utility. The 20slab "2025 데이터 총결산" mentions willingness-to-pay data, but no figures were accessible.
- I found no 2025–2026 알바천국/잡코리아 allowance survey. The 44.8만 and 51만 figures are older.

## Q2. Student B2C subscriptions and pay-per-use: what has worked (payer structures a, b)

### Takeaway
Pure B2C student subscriptions do work, but only at low conversion rates and in categories with clear outcomes such as exams or homework. The global leader converts about 9% of monthly actives to paid. Chegg shows that an answer-giving subscription can collapse once free AI arrives. In Korea, 콴다 had to build revenue mainly on tutoring and ads alongside subscriptions, and is still loss-making.

### Cited Findings
- **콴다 (매스프레소), 2024:**
  - Revenue 242억 (+42% YoY from 170억). Operating loss 194억, about 20% smaller than the prior year. — [와우테일](https://wowtale.net/2025/04/11/239695/)
  - The growth drivers were 1:1 online tutoring (콴다과외) and an online academy in Vietnam, not the subscription. — [AI타임스](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=169546)
  - Revenue mix in 2023 was about ads : subscription : tutoring = 1 : 2 : 4. This comes from a secondary analysis blog, so treat it as indicative. — [지구인사이드](https://g9inside.com/?p=54369)
- **콴다 prices:** 콴다 프리미엄 lists at **18,500원 a month** (16,500원 via KT). 프리미엄 Lite lists at **11,000원** (9,000원 via KT). Premium includes unlimited AI problem solving and 10,000 coins a month for 1:1 teacher questions, so it is a hybrid of subscription and credits. — [KT 상품 페이지](https://m.product.kt.com/static/prodetail/1629/mobile/m_htmlUploadType_20251017132325.html)
- **클로바노트:** as of mid-2026 there is **no individual paid plan**. Free use is 300 minutes, or 600 with consent. Paid plans are B2B only through NAVER WORKS: Lite 20,000원 a month, Team 108,000원, Business 510,000원. — [subanana](https://subanana.com/ko/blog/clovanote-unlimited-alternatives); [NAVER WORKS pricing](https://naver.worksmobile.com/pricing/clovanote/)
- **Global benchmark, Duolingo:** paid subscribers were **9.2% of average MAU** in FY2025 (8.8% in FY2024). That is about 12.2M subscribers as of 31 Dec 2025, up 28% YoY. — [Duolingo 10-K FY2025 (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm)
- **Global benchmark, Chegg:** Q2 2025 subscription-services subscribers were about 2.6M (−40% YoY) and revenue fell 36%, amid the AI shift. — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/chegg-q2-2025-slides-reveal-36-revenue-drop-amid-strategic-pivot-to-skills-focus-93CH-4171275)
- **Global benchmark, Quizlet:** the widely cited "42% conversion" is a streak-campaign engagement metric from a Braze case study. It is not a free-to-paid rate. Do not use it. — [Braze](https://www.braze.com/customers/quizlet)

### Inferences
- A realistic free-to-paid planning assumption for a Korean student utility is **2–5%** of monthly actives. Duolingo's 9% is a best-in-class, multi-year benchmark.
- NAVER removed the individual tier of 클로바노트, a classic student lecture-recording tool, and kept only B2B pricing. Large Korean players appear to find that individual students do not monetize well, and that institutions do. This is an inference from their pricing, not a stated rationale.
- 콴다's design (a cheap Lite tier at about 1만원, a full tier at about 1.85만원, and coins for human help) is the most relevant Korean template for combining a subscription with micro-transactions.

### Gaps
- I found no published conversion rate for 콴다 or other Korean student apps.
- I found no hard evidence of which specific micro-payment prices (990원 per item, 4,900원 a month) worked for Korean students. These remain hypotheses to test.

## Q3. Success fee / 성공보수 / 환급형 (payer structure c)

### Takeaway
삼쩜삼 proves that Koreans pay readily when the fee comes out of money the service recovers for them. It took about 12% of roughly 7,000억 in filed refunds and turned profitable. In the student space, scholarship-matching (드림스폰) uses a paid membership whose value is framed against scholarship money won. This is the strongest answer to the objection that "students don't pay".

### Cited Findings
- **자비스앤빌런즈 (삼쩜삼), 2024:**
  - Revenue **862억** (+70% from 507억). Operating profit **102억**, the first annual profit, after a 111억 loss in 2023.
  - Refund filings totaled about **7,000억** in 2024, double 2023's 3,529억.
  - The fee is reported as **10–20% of the refund amount**.
  - — [유니콘팩토리](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2025040116031295138); [파이낸셜뉴스](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202504011132267690)
- **삼쩜삼 scale:** 24M cumulative sign-ups and 2조 in cumulative refund filings (Jul 2025). The company is targeting an IPO within 2–3 years. — [아시아투데이](https://www.asiatoday.co.kr/kn/view.php?key=20260311010003211)
- **드림스폰 (scholarship information, a social enterprise):**
  - Paid membership "원패스" lists at **60만원**. The crowdfunding super-early-bird price was **58,800원**.
  - Four revenue streams: the student membership, scholarship listings and ads, co-branded scholarships, and education programs.
  - The company claims top members receive an average of 500만원 in scholarships (max 4,000만원). This is a company claim and has not been verified.
  - — [오마이컴퍼니](https://www.ohmycompany.com/reward/13448)

### Inferences
- 삼쩜삼's revenue divided by refunds filed is 862억 / 7,000억 ≈ **12.3%**, which fits the stated 10–20% fee band. So a "% of money recovered" take rate of about 10–15% is socially accepted in Korea.
- Student analogs where money is recovered include:
  - missed scholarships and 국가장학금 applications;
  - refunds (tuition, dorm, 수강신청 정정);
  - 청년 정부지원금 (울산 AI subscription support, local youth benefits);
  - deposit returns;
  - 학생회비 refunds.

  For these, price as **10–15% of money recovered** or a **fixed 4,900–9,900원 per success**. The student pays nothing unless they gain.
- Success fees need a measurable cash outcome. Annoyances that only save time (scheduling, summarizing) cannot use this model.

### Gaps
- I found no audited 2025 numbers for 삼쩜삼, and no revenue or member counts for 드림스폰.
- I found no Korean service charging a success fee on scholarships or 지원금 specifically for students.

## Q4. University, department or government pays (B2B2C, payer structure d)

### Takeaway
2026 is the year Korean universities began paying for AI for every enrolled student: SNU (ChatGPT Edu, 33억) and Dongguk (35만원 a year of multi-model credits per member). Government money is large (RISE, renamed 앵커, about 2.1조; 혁신지원사업 about 1.38조) and is being redirected toward "학생 체감형" projects. This makes "the university or its 앵커사업단 pays" the most credible payer for an AI student service. The trade-offs are slow procurement and competition with free big-tech offers.

### Cited Findings
- **서울대:**
  - ChatGPT Edu for all members (faculty, students, staff), service starting June 2026. It includes Codex, agent development and the OpenAI API. — [한국대학신문](https://news.unn.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=593027)
  - Search snippets of SNU materials state a run of June 2026 to August 2027 (15 months) for enrolled or employed members, excluding those on leave. — [대학저널/dhnews](https://dhnews.co.kr/news/view/1065573113363004); [SNU 보도자료](https://www.snu.ac.kr/snunow/press?md=v&bbsidx=170942)
- **서울대 budget:** about **33억** for the education and research AI service (reported May 2026). — [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260520_0003637590)
- **동국대 "동악 플랜" (from March 2026):**
  - Every student gets generative AI worth **35만원 a year per person** in retail value.
  - Delivery is not individual accounts. It is an integrated portal with 8+ models (GPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Solar, Llama, Sonar, Gemma) and a **monthly credit allowance** per member, run by a selected vendor.
  - — [BBS 불교방송](https://news.bbsi.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4062787); [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260520_0003637590)
- **Other universities (May 2026):**
  - 연세대: partnership with Liner (partial premium features).
  - 고려대 and 이화여대: no subscription support yet, adoption under review. — [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260520_0003637590)
- **Government:**
  - From 2027, domestic (Korean) AI tools will be provided to university and graduate students for study and research.
  - 125억 in 2026 goes to online and offline AI learning environments, and the "우리의 AI 러닝" platform launched July 2026. — [한국대학신문](https://news.unn.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=591110)
- **Local government:** 울산시 subsidizes generative-AI subscriptions for 1,000 young residents aged 19–39, up to **10만원 each**. — [울산MBC](https://www.usmbc.co.kr/NewsArticle/840792)
- **RISE budget:** 2026 RISE is **2조1,403억**, up from 1조9,410억. 4,000억 is a performance-based incentive pool used for differentiated allocation. — [뉴스핌](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260402000272)
- **RISE renamed:** RISE is now "앵커", restructured toward **학생 체감형** projects. — [한국대학신문](https://news.unn.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=591242)
- **대학·전문대학 혁신지원사업 2026:** **1조3,808억** in total. **8,191억** goes to 141 four-year universities and 5,617억 to 116 colleges, including 850억 and 340억 in specialization incentives. — [네이트/뉴스 2026-03-30](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260330n32145)
- **Kookmin precedent:** **국민대 앵커사업단** already funds AI projects with industry. In one, students used NC AI's VARCO to produce generative-AI video content (May–Sep 2026). — [네이트 뉴스 2026-09-21](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260921n14006)

### Inferences
- The average 혁신지원사업 allocation per four-year university is 8,191억 / 141 ≈ **58억 a year**. A student-service AI license of 2,000만–5,000만원 a year is under 1% of that one program, before counting 앵커/RISE money.
- SNU per-user cost, as a rough estimate: assume about 35,000 eligible users. This headcount is my assumption and is not sourced. Then 33억 / 35,000 ≈ 9.4만원 per user over 15 months, or about **6,000원 per user per month** for a full ChatGPT Edu seat. A narrower annoyance tool should therefore price well below that: about **1,000–3,000원 per student per year**, or a flat campus license.
- Dongguk's credit-based, multi-vendor portal shows universities prefer **usage-metered, pooled credits** to per-seat accounts. A startup can plug into such portals as an "app" or position itself as a vendor.
- The "students don't pay" objection becomes "the university pays out of 앵커/혁신지원 budgets earmarked for student experience". For a Kookmin hackathon, naming 국민대 앵커사업단 as the launch customer is concrete and credible.

### Gaps
- I found no public per-seat price for ChatGPT Edu in Korea, and no SNU headcount used in the contract.
- I found no published share of RISE/앵커 or 혁신지원 budgets spent on AI tools.
- I found no Kookmin-specific AI subscription support program.

## Q5. Student organizations pay: 학생회, 학과, 동아리 (payer structure e)

### Takeaway
Student-council money is small and shrinking. A large university's central council runs on about 2,500만원 a semester, and payment rates range from under 1% to about 40%. 학과 councils collect 20만–30만원 per freshman as a one-time fee. Clubs receive about 30만원 a semester from the school. Organizations can pay at most tens of thousands of won a month. They are better used as a distribution channel and a sales proof-point than as the main revenue source.

### Cited Findings
- **Payment rates (2025):**
  - 인천대 1st semester: **0.72%**, about 100 of roughly 12,000 students.
  - 이화여대 2nd semester: **24.1%**, total about 2,500만원.
  - 인하대: **1만원 per semester**, with payment rates of 42.6% and 38.4%.
  - — [경인일보 2026-04](https://www.kyeongin.com/article/1761473)
- **Council budgets:**
  - 이화여대 총학생회 2026-1 provisional budget: about **2,477만원**. A 2nd-semester promotion produced a 2,700만원 surplus. — [이대학보](https://inews.ewha.ac.kr/news/articleViewAmp.html?idxno=74840)
  - 부산대 총학생회: **2,431.5만원** collected in the first half of the year. — [채널PNU](https://channelpnu.pusan.ac.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=37155)
- **학과 학생회비:** usually collected from freshmen in early March, often 4 years at once. Typical amounts are in the **mid-20만원 range**, rising toward **30만원**. Non-payers sometimes face exclusion from events. 학과 councils are often outside formal audit. — [창원대신문](http://mpress.changwon.ac.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=3051634); [헤럴드경제](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/1967318) (date not confirmed)
- **Club funding:** school support for clubs averaged about **30만원** across 8 Seoul universities. 성신여대 gave 0원. By division, academic and religious clubs get about 15만원 a semester and performance clubs 30–35만원. — [오마이뉴스](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0001630932) (article date not confirmed, likely pre-2024)
- **Club dues (anecdotal):** commonly about 1만–3만원 per semester. — [에펨코리아 community thread](https://www.fmkorea.com/4912551284) (anecdotal, low reliability)

### Inferences
- **Willingness-to-pay ceiling, organizations:**
  - A central 총학생회 (about 2,500만원 a semester) could plausibly spend **10만–50만원 a semester** on a tool that reduces 행사·회계·명부 chores. That is 0.4–2% of budget.
  - A 학과 학생회 pool (for example, 100 freshmen × 25만원 ≈ 2,500만원 over 4 years) could pay **3만–10만원 a semester**.
  - A club could pay about **1만–3만원 a month**.
- The payment-rate collapse is itself a pain point. A service that makes council finances transparent (automated 회계 공개, receipts, dues collection) addresses the trust problem that causes low payment, so the council has a reason to pay for it.
- Organizations decide quickly: one 회장 or 집행부 approves, with no procurement. That makes them good first customers to show "someone paid" during a hackathon, even if lifetime value is small.

### Gaps
- I found no national statistics on total 학생회비 or 동아리 budgets. All figures are from individual universities.
- I found no data on what student councils currently spend on software.

## Q6. Advertisers, recruiters and brands pay; plus case studies of 에브리타임, 대학내일, 링커리어 (payer structure f)

### Takeaway
Advertising and recruiting to students is a proven Korean model, but it monetizes thinly. 에브리타임 reaches about 250만 MAU yet made only about 104억 revenue and a 2.4% operating margin in 2024. That is roughly 4,000원 per user per year. The biggest money sits with agencies that sell student access to brands: 대학내일 had about 923억 revenue. An ad or lead-gen model needs massive scale. For a hackathon startup, it works only as a secondary stream.

### Cited Findings
- **비누랩스 (에브리타임), 2024:**
  - Revenue **103억6,616만원**, up more than 5억 YoY.
  - Operating profit **2억4,734만원**, about one-eighth of the prior year. Operating margin **2.39%**, down 17.37pp.
  - Net income 9억4,345만원, including non-operating gains.
  - Fees paid (지급수수료) rose from 4.5억 to 12.4억.
  - Services: 에브리타임, 에브리유 (a student discount mall), 캠퍼스픽 (careers and 대외활동), 대학백과.
  - MAU was 2.73M (Nov 2023) with a 3.2M peak in March.
  - Exploring an IPO or SI investment and has hired Samil PwC. No outside funding to date.
  - — [머니투데이 2026-01-01](https://www.mt.co.kr/stock/2026/01/01/2025123115275364446)
- **에브리타임 ad rates (via ad agencies, not official rate cards; date unclear):**
  - Home banner averages **500만–1,000만원 per 7 days**, with a base of about **700만원 per 7 days**.
  - Agency "viral" posts cost about **2만원 per school per upload**.
  - Agencies cite an average of 251만 monthly students and 837만 cumulative sign-ups.
  - — [유니브립](https://univlip.com/%EC%97%90%EB%B8%8C%EB%A6%AC%ED%83%80%EC%9E%84-%EA%B4%91%EA%B3%A0%ED%95%98%EA%B3%A0-%EC%8B%B6%EB%8B%A4%EB%A9%B4-2%EA%B0%80%EC%A7%80-%EB%B0%A9%EB%B2%95-%EB%B0%8F-%EB%B9%84%EC%9A%A9-%EB%B0%B0/); [AMPM 인사이트](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/59747)
- **(주)대학내일, 2024:** revenue **922.6억**, operating profit **24.8억**. Its businesses are youth media, youth marketing solutions and the 20대연구소 research arm. Group-level (대학내일ES) revenue was 1,639억. — [사람인 재무정보](https://www.saramin.co.kr/zf_user/company-info/view-inner-finance/csn/cytmdWpHT0xZdTV5MDhZVE5BZUgzUT09/company_nm/(%EC%A3%BC)%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EB%82%B4%EC%9D%BC)
- **링커리어 (대외활동, 공모전, 채용), 2024:** revenue **54.5억**, about 72 employees, MAU about 50만. The model is listings and ads from large companies and public institutions. — [혁신의숲](https://www.innoforest.co.kr/company/CP00011360/%EB%A7%81%EC%BB%A4%EB%A6%AC%EC%96%B4); [사람인](https://www.saramin.co.kr/zf_user/company-info/view/csn/1058757696/company_nm/(%EC%A3%BC)%EC%8A%A4%ED%8E%99%EC%97%85%EC%95%A0%EB%93%9C?nomo=1)

### Inferences
- 에브리타임 revenue per MAU is about 103.7억 / 2.5–2.7M ≈ **3,800–4,100원 per user per year**, roughly 330원 a month. That comes from a near-monopoly campus app, which means ads alone cannot fund an AI service whose inference cost per user is likely higher.
- 대학내일 (agency, about 923억) and 링커리어 (listings, about 54억) show that **brands and recruiters pay to reach students**. "Sponsored placement inside a solved annoyance" can be a secondary stream once a service reaches tens of thousands of users at one or a few campuses. Examples: a 공모전 deadline reminder carrying a recruiter listing, or a 자취 checklist carrying a moving or furniture partner.
- Contextual lead-gen pays better than banners. Revenue-share or CPA with 자취·이사·통신·카드 partners, or with recruiting platforms, is the realistic version of payer structure (f) for a small team.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025 financials for 비누랩스, 링커리어 or 대학내일, and no revenue breakdown by ads vs. commerce for 에브리타임.
- I found no market-size estimate for the Korean "대학생 마케팅 시장".

## Q7. Parents pay; freemium plus data or lead-gen (payer structures g, h)

### Takeaway
There is little direct evidence that parents pay for university-student services in Korea. Parents fully fund the allowance for only about a quarter of students, so "parent pays" fits only high-stakes items such as scholarships, career and admissions-adjacent services. Freemium is standard practice, but given the AI cost structure the free tier must be capped by credits.

### Cited Findings
- **Who funds the allowance:** 26.1% of students are fully parent-funded, 47.0% mixed and 26.9% self-funded (dated survey). — [베리타스알파](http://www.veritas-a.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=501206)
- **Credit-capped freemium is the norm in Korean student AI:**
  - Dongguk provides monthly AI credits. — [BBS](https://news.bbsi.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4062787)
  - 콴다 bundles 10,000 coins a month into Premium. — [KT 상품 페이지](https://m.product.kt.com/static/prodetail/1629/mobile/m_htmlUploadType_20251017132325.html)
  - 클로바노트 caps free use at 300–600 minutes. — [subanana](https://subanana.com/ko/blog/clovanote-unlimited-alternatives)
- **Usage caps are a pain point:** students complain that university-provided AI has usage limits that make it ineffective. — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026090321433860550)

### Inferences
- Parents are a realistic payer only when the outcome is money or credentials (scholarships, certification, career). They could be targeted with a "gift or pay on behalf" option such as a KakaoPay request (송금 요청). This is untested.
- The "free tier + paid top-up credits" design aligns cost with use and lets heavy users self-select into paying. That makes it the right default for AI inference costs.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean data on parents paying for university-student digital services.
- I found no evidence on student-data monetization. It is also legally sensitive under PIPA and is not recommended.

## Q8. Payment friction: payment methods and subscription fatigue

### Takeaway
Payment rails are not the bottleneck. Simple payment is ubiquitous, and people in their 20s lean toward KakaoPay and Toss, which suits per-transaction or 송금-style collection, for example for club dues. The real friction is subscription fatigue: about 1만원 a month is perceived as expensive for an extra subscription.

### Cited Findings
- **Overall usage (Opensurvey 2025):** 네이버페이 64.7%, 카카오페이 49.8%, 토스페이 31.1%.
- **Transfers:** 카카오페이 45.2% and 토스페이 32.6%, vs 네이버페이 22.3%. People in their 20s lean toward 카카오페이 and 토스페이. The top reason for using KakaoPay is "the other person uses it" (38.9%), a network effect.
- — [오픈서베이 블로그](https://blog.opensurvey.co.kr/article/ds-payment-2025-2/); a 2026 follow-up likewise reports 네이버페이 first for payments and Kakao/Toss strong in transfers. — [서울경제TV 2026-07](https://www.sentv.co.kr/article/view/sentv202607050012)
- **Subscription fatigue:**
  - Ad-tier adoption rose to 34.8%, and the perceived fair single-subscription price is 7,939원. — [이데일리](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=02217286642398848)
  - "구독료 1만 원도 아깝다". — [아시아경제 2026-03](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032000181152827)

### Inferences
- For organization payers (clubs, 학과), integrate collection through KakaoPay or Toss transfer links. The tool can also *collect* dues for the organization, which is itself an annoyance it solves, and take a small fee.
- For B2C, prefer one-off or credit purchases (no recurring commitment) or semester passes that match the academic calendar, over monthly auto-renewal.

### Gaps
- I found no student-specific (as opposed to 20s-wide) payment-method data.

## Q9. Synthesis: ranked payer structures and KRW price anchors

### Takeaway
The most credible "who pays" answers for a student-annoyance AI service are, in order:
1. **The university or 앵커사업단 pays** (B2B2C).
2. **The student pays only out of money recovered** (success fee).
3. **The student organization pays** (fast but small).
4. **Partner or advertiser pays per lead.**
5. **The student pays a low-priced, credit-based B2C fee.**
6. **Parents pay.**

The strongest pitch combines 1 or 2 with a credit-based B2C fallback.

### Cited Findings (anchors used below)
- University spend precedents:
  - SNU 33억 for ChatGPT Edu for all members. — [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260520_0003637590)
  - Dongguk 35만원 a year of AI per member, via credits. — [BBS](https://news.bbsi.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4062787)
- Government pools:
  - 혁신지원사업 8,191억 for 141 universities. — [네이트](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260330n32145)
  - RISE/앵커 2.14조. — [뉴스핌](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260402000272)
- Success-fee precedent: 삼쩜삼 fee of 10–20% of the refund; 862억 revenue on about 7,000억 in refunds filed. — [유니콘팩토리](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2025040116031295138)
- Student organization budgets: 총학 about 2,500만원 a semester; 인하대 1만원 per semester per student. — [경인일보](https://www.kyeongin.com/article/1761473); [이대학보](https://inews.ewha.ac.kr/news/articleViewAmp.html?idxno=74840)
- B2C ceilings:
  - Perceived fair single-subscription price 7,939원. — [이데일리](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=02217286642398848)
  - 콴다 Lite 11,000원 and Premium 18,500원. — [KT](https://m.product.kt.com/static/prodetail/1629/mobile/m_htmlUploadType_20251017132325.html)
  - Free Google AI Pro (normally 29,000원) for students. — [파이낸셜뉴스](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202508090900587591)
- Ad benchmark: 에브리타임 about 104억 revenue on about 250만 MAU; banner about 700만원 per week. — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/stock/2026/01/01/2025123115275364446); [유니브립](https://univlip.com/%EC%97%90%EB%B8%8C%EB%A6%AC%ED%83%80%EC%9E%84-%EA%B4%91%EA%B3%A0%ED%95%98%EA%B3%A0-%EC%8B%B6%EB%8B%A4%EB%A9%B4-2%EA%B0%80%EC%A7%80-%EB%B0%A9%EB%B2%95-%EB%B0%8F-%EB%B9%84%EC%9A%A9-%EB%B0%B0/)

### Inferences: ranked payer structures

All prices below are proposals derived from the anchors above, not observed market prices.

1. **University, college or 앵커사업단 pays (B2B2C license).**
   - *Evidence:* SNU and Dongguk paid for all-student AI in 2026. Government budgets (혁신지원 about 58억 per university per year on average; 앵커 2.14조) are being steered toward student experience. 국민대 앵커사업단 already funds student AI projects.
   - *Price anchors:*
     - Pilot or PoC: **500만–2,000만원 per semester** for one college or department.
     - Campus license: **2,000만–5,000만원 a year**, or **1,000–3,000원 per enrolled student per year**. Compare SNU's full ChatGPT Edu seat at roughly 6,000원 per user per month; this estimate uses an assumed headcount.
   - *Risks:* procurement cycles of 6–12 months; big tech gives general AI free.
   - *Pitch:* sell an outcome, not "AI access". Examples: fewer 행정 inquiries, 수강신청 or 졸업요건 errors avoided, staff hours saved.
2. **Success fee: the student pays only from money recovered.**
   - *Evidence:* the 삼쩜삼 model (about 12% effective take, profitable at 862억 revenue) and 드림스폰 (membership framed against scholarship winnings).
   - *Price anchors:* **10–15% of recovered amount**, capped (for example at 3만원), or a flat **4,900–9,900원 per success**. Examples: a scholarship won, a refund received, a 지원금 granted, a deposit returned.
   - *Strength:* it directly defeats the "students don't pay" objection, because the student is never out of pocket.
3. **Student organization pays (학생회, 학과, 동아리).**
   - *Evidence:* 총학 budgets of about 2,500만원 a semester, 학과 pools from 20만–30만원 freshman fees, clubs on about 30만원 a semester of school support. Payment-rate crises motivate tools that improve transparency.
   - *Price anchors:*
     - Club: **9,900–29,000원 a month**.
     - 학과 학생회: **3만–10만원 a semester**.
     - 총학 or 단과대: **10만–50만원 a semester**.
     - Alternatively, **1–3% of dues collected** through the tool.
   - *Role:* first paying customers and channel, not the main revenue.
4. **Partners or advertisers pay per lead, placed contextually inside the solved task.**
   - *Evidence:* 대학내일 at about 923억 and 링커리어 at about 54억 show brands and recruiters pay. 에브리타임 shows banners alone yield only about 4,000원 per user per year.
   - *Price anchors:* CPA or revenue share with 자취, 통신, 금융, 채용 partners. Banner-scale ad income needs 10만+ MAU; benchmark 에브리타임 at about 700만원 per week for a nationwide home banner.
5. **Student B2C, credit-based freemium.**
   - *Evidence:* 30.1% of 20s AI users pay. Heavy-AI students pay about 3만원 a month for general AI. Apps and software are about 2만원 of a 68만원 monthly budget. The fair-price ceiling is about 7,939원. Free Google and Perplexity offers exist. Duolingo converts about 9% at best.
   - *Price anchors:*
     - **990–1,900원 per task** (credit packs, e.g. 10 for 9,900원).
     - **2,900–4,900원 a month**.
     - **9,900–14,900원 per semester pass** timed to 시험기간 or 수강신청.
     - Stay below 콴다 Lite (11,000원) and Google AI Plus (11,000원).
     - Plan on 2–5% conversion.
6. **Parents pay.** Weak evidence. Only for high-stakes outcomes (scholarships, careers), using a KakaoPay or Toss "pay on behalf" request. Treat it as upside, not the plan.

### Gaps
- None of the B2C micro-price points (990원, 4,900원) are validated by a Korean student-specific study. They are extrapolated from OTT fair-price data, 콴다 pricing and big-tech free offers. The hackathon team should validate them with a quick in-campus survey or pre-sale (for example, 에브리타임 post plus a Toss payment link).
- Per-seat costs in Korean university AI contracts are not public. The university price anchors are estimates.
