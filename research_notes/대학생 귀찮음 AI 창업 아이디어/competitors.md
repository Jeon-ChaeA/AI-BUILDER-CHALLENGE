# Competitive landscape: services that fix university-life annoyances (Korea + global, 2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-03. Scope: products a Korean university student (e.g., Kookmin University) can use today, plus global benchmarks. "AI?" means the product's core value comes from AI rather than a bolted-on feature.
Data caveats: some automated summaries of Korean articles mistranslated units (만 to "million"). Where that happened, the corrected figure is given with a flag.

---

## Q0. What exists, by category? (product map)

### Takeaway
Every category has incumbents, but they split cleanly. **Content and studying** (summarise, quiz, tutor) is crowded and largely free. **Money and admin for groups** (club accounting, settlement reports, receipts) and **per-school rule-heavy admin** (graduation audit, 공결, notices) are served only by bank features, generic tools, or one-school student side projects.

- Notice bots are a **common student toy project**, built for 아주대 (KakaoTalk), 한라대, and many others with Selenium crawlers and Kakao or Telegram APIs. None became a cross-school product — [아주대 카톡봇](https://luftaquila.io/works/ajounotice/); [velog 한라대](https://velog.io/@mildsalmon/%EB%8C%80%ED%95%99%EA%B5%90-%EA%B3%B5%EC%A7%80-%EC%95%8C%EB%A6%BC-%EB%B4%87-%EC%A0%9C%EC%9E%91%EA%B8%B0-2-python-%EA%B0%9C%EB%B0%9C-ugk4se0p2f); [SCNU-Lens](https://github.com/GOBIEBERPA/scnu-lens)
- 공결 (출석인정) procedures differ by school and even by department. Some use the portal, others **email a form plus supporting documents to the college**. Deadlines are usually 2 weeks after the absence, with exceptions such as 생리공결 within 24h. Approval is at the instructor's discretion — [전남대](https://geology.jnu.ac.kr/bbs/geology/1519/910320/artclView.do); [국민대 SW학부 공결 공지](https://cs.kookmin.ac.kr/news/notice/2256); [아주대](https://www.ajou.ac.kr/kr/bachelor/class-recognition.do)
- No commercial 공결 or 휴학 paperwork assistant was found.

### Inferences
- The incumbents fall into three tiers: (1) **free horizontal AI** (ChatGPT, Gemini/NotebookLM free for a year for Korean students, Clova Note), (2) **funded vertical AI** (GPAI, 뤼튼, 라이너, 릴리스, Turbo, StudyFetch), and (3) **one-school student side projects** (척척학사, notice bots, 우학동). Tier 3 is where pain was proven but no one scaled.
- Everytime is a distribution channel as much as a competitor. 척척학사 got its first users by posting on Everytime, and AskSia piggybacks on it with an extension. Everytime itself has shipped no visible AI and its margin is collapsing, so it is unlikely to out-build a hackathon team in the next year.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 figures were found for 대학내일 (company), 위비티, 요즘것들, 한국장학재단 app, Quizlet, Notion AI student pricing, 그루비, 썸원, Presence, 애니맨, 직방/다방 대학가 features, or 캠퍼스픽 user counts.
- Everytime's 2025 financials (FY2025 filing) were not found.
- Whether Kakao Bank's "AI 모임총무" actually launched in Q4 2025, and what it does, is unconfirmed.

## Q2. Where do students still complain that nothing good exists?

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence of unmet need is students building tools themselves because the school system is "too unfriendly": graduation audits, notice alerts, and club admin. There are also persistent complaints about team-project free riders and fake housing listings. In all of these, the work is rule-, document-, or money-heavy and school-specific, which generic chatbots don't touch.

### Cited Findings
- 척척학사's founder said: "이 시스템은 너무 불친절하다… 내가 직접 확인할 수 있는 도구를 원했다". He built it after the portal required manual PDF downloads and calculation and the 학사팀 gave insufficient help. A roughly 2-hour MVP posted to Everytime got strong reactions — [대학내일](https://www.univ20.com/Magazine/Detail/2636)
- Student developers at many universities independently build notice crawlers and bots, which shows schools' push and notice systems don't satisfy them. SCNU-Lens exists because notices are scattered across 109 boards at one school — [SCNU-Lens](https://github.com/GOBIEBERPA/scnu-lens); [아주대](https://luftaquila.io/works/ajounotice/)
- Club officers describe a 5-step manual chain for every intake: Google Form → share → check bank account → match payers → invite to KakaoTalk room. Equipment is managed on paper, Excel, or Notion — [우학동](https://github.com/team8901)
- Free riders in team projects are a widely shared grievance, and community threads discuss removing free riders' names — [여성경제신문](https://www.womaneconomy.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=214519); [링커리어](https://community.linkareer.com/jayuu/4191230)
- Students rent rooms amid rising fake and bait listings (374 cases in 2025) and illegal subdivided rooms — [AP신문](https://www.apnews.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=3051521); [네이트](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260219n26201)
- 공결 often requires emailing forms and evidence to the college office, with tight deadlines (24h for 생리공결) — [전남대](https://geology.jnu.ac.kr/bbs/geology/1519/910320/artclView.do); [국민대 SW](https://cs.kookmin.ac.kr/news/notice/2256)

### Inferences
- The "귀찮음" with no good product is **procedural, not intellectual**: forms, money reconciliation, rule-checking, deadlines, chasing people. AI helps here as OCR, rule parsing, form-filling, and agentic follow-up, not as Q&A, which sidesteps the free-ChatGPT problem.
- Payers are more natural here than in study tools: club and council officers with a budget, 학생회 (accountability pressure), departments (공결 and 졸업사정 workload), and landlords or agents (verified listings).
- Kookmin specifically: ON국민 already covers timetable lookup, 휴·보강, scholarship application, and has a chatbot, so a team should avoid re-skinning those. Graduation audit and 공결 documents are not listed among ON국민's features — [국민대 정보통신처](https://kist.kookmin.ac.kr/kist_new/service/sevice02.do).

### Gaps
- No systematic Korean survey (2024–26) ranking student administrative pain points was found. Evidence is anecdotal or from builders.
- No direct student complaints about Toss or Kakao club accounts (e.g., "can't produce a settlement report") were found. This is an inferred gap, not an observed complaint.

## Q3. What did prior hackathon / 공모전 teams in Korea build for "대학생활" themes, and what got traction?

### Takeaway
Little public record exists of hackathon winners on university-life themes. The products that got real traction came from capstones and side projects that solved one school's admin pain and spread through Everytime, not from hackathon stage wins.

### Cited Findings
- **우학동**: a 2024-2 SW capstone that became a live club-admin SaaS (woohakdong.com) — [GitHub](https://github.com/team8901/woohakdong-web-legacy); [woohakdong.com](https://www.woohakdong.com/)
- **척척학사 (수원대, Apr 2025)**: a side project with 637 active users at launch coverage and a later claim of 6,000+ — [대학내일](https://www.univ20.com/Magazine/Detail/2636); [GitHub](https://github.com/cchaksa/cchaksa-backend)
- **Kookmin precedent**: a Kookmin software student's team "비트바이트" built 바른말 키패드 (cuts profanity), with 12만 downloads and about 4,000 monthly users — [이데일리](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=03276726615899728). Kookmin's SW college GitHub has 386 repositories of capstones — [GitHub kookmin-sw](https://github.com/kookmin-sw)
- Academic and student projects on roommate matching (숭실대, collaborative filtering, KIPS 2023) — [KIPS](https://www.manuscriptlink.com/society/kips/conference/ask2023/file/downloadSoConfManuscript/abs/KIPS_C2023A0291F)
- Recent AI hackathon winners skew toward career and productivity tools rather than campus admin. 2025 AI 서울 해커톤 awardee **Resume Git** (resume building for junior developers) — [링커리어](https://community.linkareer.com/strategy/4250759). Kakao "랄프톤" AI-agent hackathon winner (Oct 2026): STRIDE, a voice-guided running game, built by high-schoolers — [중도일보](https://www.joongdo.co.kr/web/view.php?key=20261002010000558)
- Turbo AI (US) began as a classroom idea by two students and reached 5M users. It is the global proof that a student-built tool can hit eight-figure ARR, but in the now-crowded notes category — [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/10/23/20-year-old-dropouts-built-ai-notetaker-turbo-ai-to-5-million-users)

### Inferences
- The traction pattern: **one school → Everytime post → word of mouth**. Expansion stalls because rules and systems differ per school. A product designed with a per-school "rule pack" that AI can generate from 학칙/요람 PDFs could break that ceiling. That is the defensible AI angle.
- A judge will likely ask "why won't ChatGPT, GPAI, or Toss just do this?" Strong answers are: school-specific data and rules, multi-party workflows (officers ↔ members ↔ school), and money or document artefacts (settlement reports, filled forms) that chatbots don't produce.

### Gaps
- No public results for prior 국민대 hackathons (e.g., earlier Kookmin AI Builder Challenge editions) or other "대학생활"-themed 공모전 winners were found. Recommend checking Kookmin's 창업지원단 or LINC notices and Devpost/aifactory directly.
- Traction numbers for most student projects are self-reported (GitHub READMEs).
